# Week 10 Work Checklist — SmartServe POS

**Team:** Team 3 — SmartServe POS  
**Week:** 10  
**Period:** November 5–11, 2026  
**Main Focus:** MVP Gap Review and Verification Preparation

## 1. Status Rules

- **Planned:** Work has not started.
- **In Progress:** Work has started, but is not complete.
- **Completed:** Work is finished and supporting evidence is attached or linked.
- **Blocked:** Work cannot continue; the reason must be documented.

**Rule:** A task cannot be marked Completed without verifiable evidence.

## 3. Evidence Naming Convention

Use clear names that identify the workflow and test:

- `mvp-menu-selection.png`
- `order-total-verification.png`
- `simulated-payment-result.png`
- `receipt-verification.png`
- `inventory-before-sale.png`
- `inventory-after-sale.png`
- `duplicate-deduction-check.png`
- `backend-test-results.txt`
- `frontend-build-results.txt`
- `independent-review-results.md`

Use actual filenames and dates when collecting evidence. Do not create placeholder screenshots or logs and present them as test results.

## 4. Completion Record

For every completed task, record:

- Task name
- Date performed
- Person responsible
- Environment used
- Relevant commit or issue, if applicable
- Evidence filename or link
- Actual result
- Any limitations or unresolved problems

## 5. Week 10 Completion Criteria

Week 10 work is ready for review when:

- The current MVP scope has been reviewed.
- Known gaps and bugs are documented.
- An end-to-end verification procedure is prepared.
- Independent verification is arranged or its blocker is documented.
- Evidence is organized and linked in the evidence index.
- The weekly report reflects actual work and verified results.

**Final rule: No evidence, no completion claim.**
