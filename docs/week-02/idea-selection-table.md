## Selected Project

### SmartServe POS

**Target users:** Owners, managers, and employees of small cafés and restaurants.

**Problem:** Small food businesses often manage orders, ingredient inventory, expenses, and daily sales separately. This makes it difficult to track stock, cash flow, and profitability accurately.

**Smallest useful version:** A web-based POS where administrators manage menu items, recipes, ingredients, and employees. Cashiers create orders and process simulated payments. Completed sales generate receipts, deduct the correct recipe ingredients from inventory, and update daily sales records.

**Main risk:** Orders, payments, and inventory must remain synchronized without missing or duplicate ingredient deductions.

## Backup Project

### Chimeki Market Nepal

**Target users:** People in Nepal who want to buy or sell used products or find nearby part-time jobs.

**Problem:** Existing platforms do not fully combine neighborhood-based trading, local job discovery, user ratings, and community safety in one place.

**Smallest useful version:** A location-based marketplace where users create product or job listings, search by category and area, save listings, message other users, leave ratings, and report suspicious content.

**Main risk:** Preventing scams, fraudulent job advertisements, unsafe meetings, and misleading listings while maintaining user trust.

## Selection Summary

Team 3 selected **SmartServe POS** as its primary project direction and **Chimeki Market Nepal** as its backup direction.

SmartServe POS was selected because its main workflow is specific, practical, and suitable for a midterm demonstration. The team can demonstrate how one completed café order affects the receipt, ingredient inventory, and daily sales dashboard.
