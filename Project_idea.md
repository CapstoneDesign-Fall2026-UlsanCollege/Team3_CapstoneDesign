# Five Project Ideas

**Team:** Team 3
**Week:** 1

## Idea 1

* **Project name:** SmartServe POS
* **Target user:** Owners and employees of small cafés and restaurants.
* **Problem:** Small food businesses often manage orders, ingredient stock, expenses, and daily sales separately, making it difficult to track inventory, cash flow, and profitability accurately.
* **Smallest useful version:** A web-based POS where administrators manage menus, recipes, ingredients, and employees. Cashiers process orders and simulated payments, while completed sales automatically update inventory and daily sales records.
* **Midterm demo could show:** A cashier completing a latte order, generating a receipt, automatically deducting milk and coffee beans from inventory, and updating the sales dashboard.
* **Big risk / unknown:** Keeping orders, payments, and inventory synchronized without incorrect or duplicate stock deductions.

## Idea 2

* **Project name:** TransferPath Korea
* **Target user:** International students in Korea who want to transfer to another college or university.
* **Problem:** Transfer requirements, available courses, deadlines, and required documents differ between institutions and are often difficult for international students to understand.
* **Smallest useful version:** A multi-college platform with Super Admin, College Admin, and Student roles. Colleges publish their courses, eligibility conditions, deadlines, and required documents. Students search programs, create personalized checklists, and send inquiries.
* **Midterm demo could show:** A student selecting a college and course, receiving a transfer-document checklist, tracking completion progress, and sending a question to the college administrator.
* **Big risk / unknown:** Colleges must regularly update their information. The first version will use verified information from our college and clearly labelled demonstration data for other institutions.

## Idea 3

* **Project name:** Chimeki Market Nepal
* **Target user:** People in Nepal who want to buy or sell used products or find nearby part-time jobs.
* **Problem:** Existing platforms do not fully combine trusted neighborhood-based trading, local job discovery, user ratings, and community safety in one place.
* **Smallest useful version:** A location-based marketplace where users create product or job listings, search by category and area, save listings, message other users, leave ratings, and report suspicious content.
* **Midterm demo could show:** A Kathmandu user finding a nearby second-hand table, messaging the seller, and discovering a local café job through the same platform.
* **Big risk / unknown:** Preventing scams, fraudulent job advertisements, unsafe meetings, and misleading product listings while maintaining user trust.

## Idea 4

* **Project name:** Streamer LiveLingo Assistant
* **Target user:** Streamers who communicate with foreign-language speakers on random video-chat or international streaming platforms.
* **Problem:** Streamers may encounter people speaking languages they do not understand, making it difficult to continue entertaining and natural conversations.
* **Smallest useful version:** A private streamer-side companion that captures conversation audio with permission, displays real-time transcription and English translation, and suggests friendly, funny, or flirty replies in the speaker’s language with English meanings and pronunciation.
* **Midterm demo could show:** A Spanish speaker asking a question, the assistant displaying its English translation, and generating three Spanish response options that only the streamer can see.
* **Big risk / unknown:** Achieving accurate transcription and fast translation despite background noise, overlapping speech, different accents, privacy requirements, and platform audio-capture limitations.

  
## Idea 5

* **Project name:** VisionGuard AI
* **Target user:** Safety supervisors in factories, construction sites, and college workshops.
* **Problem:** Manually monitoring whether every worker is wearing required safety equipment is time-consuming, and safety violations may go unnoticed or remain undocumented.
* **Smallest useful version:** A web application where a supervisor uploads a workplace image and a computer-vision model detects people, helmets, and safety vests. If a person appears to be missing required equipment, the system highlights the possible violation and stores it in a database with the detection time, missing equipment type, image reference, confidence score, location, and review status.
* **Midterm demo could show:** Uploading a workplace image, marking properly equipped workers in green and possible violations in red, saving each detected violation to the database, and displaying the records in a supervisor dashboard for review.
* **Big risk / unknown:** Poor lighting, unusual camera angles, partially hidden workers, or limited training data could create false detections. Therefore, every record should be labelled as a possible violation until a supervisor manually confirms or dismisses it.
